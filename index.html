Graham Value · version

How it works

Notes

The formula

Value = EPS × (8.5 + 2g) × 4.4 / Y

  • EPS: profit per share over the last 12 months.
  • 8.5: the P/E Graham thought a company with no growth deserved.
  • 2g: 2 extra points of P/E for each 1% of expected yearly growth.
  • 4.4 / Y: 4.4% was the US AAA bond yield in Graham's day. When bonds pay more than that, stocks must be cheaper to compete.

The reel's example (Alphabet, Oct 2026): 11.30 × (8.5 + 2 × 19.21) × 4.4 / 6.11 ≈ $382.

Why two values

8.5 + 2g was set for 1960s US stocks and is generous. A company growing 5% gets a P/E of 18.5. The conservative 7 + 1g is a common investor tweak, not Graham's own rule. For Maybank in Oct 2026, the original gave RM16.24 and the conservative RM10.54, against a price near RM10 and analyst targets near RM11.8.

Graham Number = √(22.5 × EPS × book value per share). It needs no growth guess and works as a cross-check.

Bursa stocks

  • EPS is reported in sen. Add the last four quarters. The app converts to RM.
  • Use core EPS. Remove revaluation and fair-value gains, disposal gains, forex swings and big impairments.
  • Y is Bank Negara's 10-yr AAA yield (4.36% on 20 Oct 2026). It's close to 4.4, so the bond adjustment is about 1.0 right now.
  • Most large caps grow EPS 3–8% a year. Above 15%, lean on the conservative value.
  • Where to look: quarterly reports on bursamalaysia.com, i3investor, KLSE Screener, research house reports.

International stocks

  • Match Y to the currency the company earns in. USD uses Moody's Aaa (6.11% on 19 Oct 2026).
  • Keep EPS and price in the same currency. Watch ADRs, where one share may equal several local shares.
  • US "adjusted" EPS often leaves out stock-based pay, which is a real cost.
  • High-growth stocks: g drives the answer. Test lower g in the sensitivity table.

Where it doesn't fit

  • Cyclicals (O&G services, plantations, gloves, steel, chips): use average EPS across a full cycle.
  • REITs: use distribution yield against the government bond yield.
  • Banks: check P/B and ROE too.
  • Loss-makers: the formula needs positive, steady EPS.

A screening tool, not investment advice or a price target. Check every input against the company's own reports.
